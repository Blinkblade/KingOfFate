P4 Gate 7 — scripted-dummy experiment (2026-09-21)
==================================================

Setup
-----
Three runtime-only dummies generated from game/chars/_template by
tests/p4/make_dummy.ps1. The only difference between them is what their
[StateDef -3] (runs every tick) asserts:

  test_dummy_plain   (no assert)                <- control
  test_dummy_guard   assertSpecial{flag: autoGuard}
  test_dummy_jump    assertInput{flag: U}

Both sctrls are copied from the engine's own data/training.zss (:82-83 and
:204-209), i.e. this is exactly how the built-in Training mode drives its dummy.

P1 is always test_fighter_b with -p1.ai 0; the projectile is fired by injecting
236 + x with tests/p2/inject_phases.ps1:

  -Phases '0x28:0.06,0x28+0x27:0.06,0x27:0.06,0x09:0.7' -SettleSec 0.02 -NoBurst

Every number below was read by tools/read_frame_text.py from the P2 row of the
debug overlay (P2; 57; LIF:....). Nothing here is eyeballed.

Results (P2 LIF)
----------------

                     before injection      after projectile      delta
  plain  (control)        1000                   940              -60
  guard  (autoGuard)      1000                   994               -6
  jump   (hold U)         1000                   940              -60

Conclusion
----------
- plain vs guard is a clean A/B: 60 damage becomes 6. Guard engages. Gate 7
  "projectile guarded" is now PASS with machine evidence.
- jump is identical to the control, so it proves nothing: holding "up" makes the
  dummy jump repeatedly, and at the moment the projectile arrives it is most
  likely back on the ground. Gate 7 "projectile jumped over" stays BLOCKED until
  the dummy is airborne at the impact tick (next step: framestep_probe to hold
  the dummy in its jump state while the projectile passes).
